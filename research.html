Main project

Mapping Lebanon's solar PV installations from satellite imagery

A transfer-learning segmentation pipeline, built from raw satellite scenes to a validated national inventory. Final-year project at the GeoAI Research Group, CNRS-L. Supervisor: Dr. Ali J. Ghandour.

  • UNet++ with focal Tversky and boundary-aware losses, on 0.87 m VISION-1 imagery.
  • Satellite preprocessing: orthorectification, pan-sharpening, clipping.
  • Geospatial post-processing and national-scale inference.
  • Created the first Lebanese solar-panel segmentation dataset: 800 image-mask tiles.
  • 30+ training experiments. Literature review of 30+ papers.
  • Validated against national energy reference data.
